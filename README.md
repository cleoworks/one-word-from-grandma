## The word

> **Ribot**

The entry is one word: the word a grandma would text about AI. "Ribot" is how she says "robot" — one vowel off, warm and a little baffled, and it can only be about a machine. No sentence, no explanation, no punctuation tricks.

## Why this word

A grandma does not text "robot" — she texts what she hears, and what she hears is "ribot". The misspelling is the whole joke: it sounds like her voice, it is funny without a caption, and it points straight at a machine. It is also a word nobody else in the field had used when it was picked.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` is the entrypoint: it writes `Ribot` to `/output/entry.txt` (falling back to `output/entry.txt` when run outside the grading sandbox) and prints it. The file is 5 bytes of UTF-8, inside the 1–30 character gate.
